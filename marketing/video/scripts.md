# Short-form video scripts — DRAFT (20–45 s)

## 1. "Do you know your real profit this month?"
HOOK (0–3 s): "Your sales were great this month. So why is there no money?"
PROBLEM (3–10 s): Sales aren't profit, and profit isn't cash.
SHOW (10–30 s): Type three rows on TRANSACTIONS → cut to Dashboard: revenue, expenses, net profit, cash card update.
RESULT (30–38 s): "Now you know exactly where it went."
CTA: "SimpleLedger — link in bio."

## 2. "Record a sale in 20 seconds"
Screen only: date → Money In → Product Sales → Cash on Hand → amount → ✓ OK appears → P&L updates.

## 3. "Who owes you money?"
Hook: "That invoice you forgot is a free loan to your customer." Show AR sheet: Overdue status, aging chart, "due in next 14 days".
