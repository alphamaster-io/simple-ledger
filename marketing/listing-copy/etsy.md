## FAQ
- **Do I need to know accounting?** No. Choose "Money In" or "Money Out" and a category; the workbook does the debits and credits.
- **Does it calculate my taxes?** It tracks VAT/GST collected and paid and gives you the profit figure your tax return needs. It does not file taxes.
- **Can I change the categories?** Yes — add, rename or deactivate accounts on the Chart of Accounts sheet.
- **Multiple businesses?** Use one copy per business (your license allows this for your own businesses — see terms).
- **Macros?** None. Safe to open, no security warnings.
- **Can I print?** Every report is set up to print or save as PDF.
