# Support FAQ & Reply Templates

Every repeated question should become documentation, a UX fix, or a feature (master plan §46). Log each ticket's category in the monthly review.

| Category | Typical question | Reply (template) | Product follow-up |
|---|---|---|---|
| Download | "I can't find my file" | Etsy: Purchases → Download files. The zip contains Excel/, Examples/, Guides/. Re-sent here: [link] | Confirm the delivery email copy |
| Compatibility | "Does it work on Mac / Excel 2016 / Google Sheets?" | Built for Excel 2010+ on Windows and Mac and Microsoft 365. No macros. [Google Sheets: answer per the Phase 10 result] | Keep the compatibility list in the listing current |
| Formula issue | "I see #REF! / numbers look wrong" | 1) Look at the Dashboard's Data Health box. 2) Check the ⛔ rows. 3) If a grey cell was typed over, copy the formula from the row above or restore your backup. Send a screenshot of DATA HEALTH if it persists | Is protection strong enough? |
| Setup | "Where do opening balances go?" | Cash and bank on SETUP. Everything else (loans, equipment, stock) on COA → Opening Balance. The workbook balances them automatically | Add to Quick Start |
| Accounting concept | "Why isn't my equipment purchase in expenses?" | Equipment is an asset, so it's on the Balance Sheet. Use Depreciation adjustments to expense it over time (HELP → How to record expenses) | Blog / social post |
| Customization | "Can I add categories?" | Yes. Add a row on COA with a unique name and a type. Reports and dropdowns update automatically | — |
| Credit sales | "Customer paid but invoice still unpaid" | The Customer Payment's Reference must equal the invoice # exactly, and its date must be on or before the report month's end | Consider a dropdown of open invoices |
| Refund | "I want a refund" | Per policy: [policy]. Ask what didn't work, to learn from it | Tag the reason |
