# Product Positioning & Customer Profile

## Positioning statement

For **small business owners and freelancers who run their finances in spreadsheets or notebooks**, **SimpleLedger** is an **Excel bookkeeping system** that turns everyday entries into a profit & loss, balance sheet, cash flow and receivables/payables view, and **checks itself** so mistakes can't silently corrupt the numbers. Unlike income/expense trackers, it is real double-entry accounting. Unlike QuickBooks-class software, it is a one-time purchase that needs no accounting knowledge.

**Tagline options:** "Bookkeeping that checks itself." · "Know your real profit, not just your sales." · "Real accounting. No accountant required."

## Ideal customer profile

**Primary: micro business owner (1–10 people).** Retail shop, food business, online seller, home business, service provider, contractor, small agency.

- Already uses Excel (or would). Price-sensitive; one-time purchase preferred over subscriptions.
- Understands sales and costs, not debits and credits.
- Triggers to buy: tax season, a loan or grant application that asks for financial statements, "sales are up but cash is down", an unpaid invoice that hurt.
- Markets where VAT/GST tracking is a strong differentiator: Philippines, UK, Australia, New Zealand, EU, South Africa, Singapore.

**Secondary:** bookkeepers / virtual assistants (multi-client: one workbook per client), accounting students (learning double entry with a working model), business coaches (recommend it to clients; affiliate channel).

## Problem → promise

| Customer says | We promise | Proof in the product |
|---|---|---|
| "I don't know where my money went" | See every peso/dollar by category | P&L by month, top expenses chart, account ledger |
| "Sales are high but I have no cash" | See cash separately from profit | Cash KPI, cash flow statement, AR aging |
| "Customers owe me and I lose track" | Never forget an unpaid invoice | AR register, overdue status, due-in-14-days |
| "I'm scared I'll mess up the formulas" | It warns you instead of being wrong | Check column, protected reports, self-balancing checks |
| "Accounting software is too complicated/expensive" | One file, one price, no learning curve | 3 input sheets, dropdowns, Help sheet |

## Differentiators (ranked)

1. **Self-checking books.** Trial balance, balance sheet and cash-flow reconciliations, plus a Dashboard data-health panel.
2. **Real statements.** Balance sheet and direct cash flow, not only income/expense.
3. **AR/AP with partial payments and aging.**
4. **Optional VAT/GST** (input vs output tax).
5. **No macros, works on Excel 2010+.**

## What we deliberately don't claim

Tax filing, payroll, inventory costing, bank feeds, multi-currency, BIR/HMRC/IRS certification, Google Sheets support (until tested).
