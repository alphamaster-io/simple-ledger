## 2. Price range

- Paid marketplace templates cluster around **$10–$30**, with frequent "sale" pricing (list price about 1.6× the sale price). Outliers run from about $4 to about $48.
- The software substitute costs about **$456 a year** (QBO Simple Start, $38/mo). A one-time workbook at $19–$29 is under 7% of one year of software.

## 3. Common features (table stakes)

Single entry tab feeding reports · monthly P&L · annual summary · dashboard with charts · income/expense categories · Excel **and** Google Sheets · instructions tab.

## 4. Gaps and differentiation opportunities

| Gap seen in the category | SimpleLedger answer |
|---|---|
| Most "bookkeeping" templates are income/expense trackers. Few have a real balance sheet, so the numbers can't be checked | True double entry under the hood: trial balance, balance sheet and cash-flow reconciliation all self-check |
| Errors are silent (a typo becomes a wrong report) | Check column on every row; bad rows are excluded and counted on the Dashboard |
| Cash vs profit confusion ("sales are high but no cash") | Direct-method cash flow, AR/AP aging, cash KPI next to profit |
| No VAT/GST handling, or US-sales-tax-only | Optional VAT/GST tracking (output vs input tax) for PH, UK, AU, EU and similar markets |
| Credit sales and unpaid bills handled as afterthoughts | First-class AR and AP registers with partial payments, terms, aging, overdue |
| Macros, or modern-only functions that break on older Excel | No macros; Excel 2010+ function set |

## 5. Customer pain points (from comparison articles and forums)

- Software (QuickBooks and similar) feels too complex or too expensive for simple, predictable businesses. Spreadsheets "still make sense" for them ([SaaSAnt](https://saasant.com/blog/quickbooks-vs-excel/), [Fortune](https://fortune.com/article/should-you-upgrade-to-quickbooks-online/)).
- The main criticism of spreadsheets is manual entry and **formula errors**. This is the risk SimpleLedger is designed to remove (validation, self-checks, protected formulas).
- In the Philippines, many micro businesses keep manual books. BIR rules govern books of accounts, and non-VAT taxpayers commonly use manual books ([Triple i Consulting](https://www.tripleiconsulting.com/?p=6541)). **Implication:** position SimpleLedger as *management* bookkeeping and a source for the books, **not** as a BIR-registered computerized accounting system, unless that is formally pursued.

## 6. Recommendations

- **Positioning:** "The Excel bookkeeping system that checks itself." It is real accounting (P&L, balance sheet, cash flow, AR/AP) without needing to know accounting.
- **Launch price hypothesis:** Standard at **$24** (anchor $39, launch sale $19); Free tracker as lead magnet; Premium at $49 later. Test it, don't assume it (see `launch-plan.md`).
- **Keyword starter list:** small business accounting excel template · bookkeeping spreadsheet · profit and loss template · income and expense tracker · balance sheet template · cash flow spreadsheet · accounts receivable tracker · invoice tracker excel · VAT tracker excel · bookkeeping template Philippines.
